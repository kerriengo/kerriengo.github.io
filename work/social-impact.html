the brief

social impact work puts partners and access to care first: suicide prevention, caregivers, scholarships for new therapists, veterans, giving and breast cancer awareness. it also holds the permission project, our world mental health day 2026 campaign built on ten unwritten rules women inherit.

my approach

i started with who each partner serves, then wrote for them. partner and clinician voices lead, and the tone stays warm and plain, with gratitude and no pressure.

permission project result

launch window, oct 8 to 9, organic and promoted across four platforms: 19 posts, 214,380 video views, 3,680 engagements and a 1.99% average engagement rate. source: dash social campaign summary.

the rewrite guide is an educational reflection workbook, not a replacement for mental health care.
