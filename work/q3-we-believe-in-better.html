the brief

we believe in better starts from one truth: believing in mental health care is easy, acting on it is harder. it speaks to people who back therapy but face barriers like cost, stigma, finding the right therapist and fitting care into real life.

my approach

i met people where the hesitation lives. hang-xiety, sunshine guilt, summer burnout and first in my bloodline give words to feelings people rarely say out loud. awareness days and collabs with March of Dimes and SHOUT UK made room for communities whose stories often go unheard, and the Reddit AMAs let therapists answer real questions in public. the through-line: better is something you keep doing, at your own pace.

result

instagram, september 2026 vs august, across the account: accounts engaged up 3.5x (71.5K to 251.2K), shares up 511%, saves up 78%. collab posts led the month, and the top one earned 54.5K organic engagements. source: monthly organic social recap.
