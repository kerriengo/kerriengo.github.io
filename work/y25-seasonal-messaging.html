the brief

the 2025 holiday season positioned therapy as a simple, flexible check-in, not something to put off until the new year. it speaks to people juggling year-end deadlines, family dynamics and hard feelings.

my approach

the season tells everyone to feel happy, and many people don't. i wrote for the quiet versions of that: shorter days, family boundaries, grief, the pressure to be grateful. anti sad routine, holiday boundaries and home can be a person name the feeling first, then offer one small comfort. the tone stays warm and low pressure.
