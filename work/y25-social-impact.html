the brief

social impact collaborations from fall 2025 put partners and access to care first: suicide prevention month, caregivers and Alzheimer's awareness, scholarships for new therapists, veterans and giving campaigns.

my approach

i started with who each partner serves, then wrote for them: people affected by suicide, caregivers of people with Alzheimer's, aspiring therapists facing barriers to the profession, veterans and their families. partner and clinician voices lead. the tone stays warm and plain, with gratitude and no pressure.
