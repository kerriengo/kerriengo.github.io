year

the brief

with you offers real human support, on your terms, at your pace. posts move people from awareness to connection to action: name a feeling, validate it, show how therapy can help. it speaks to people who look fine on the outside but feel off, lonely or behind.

my approach

this campaign is for people who can't put a name to what they feel. quiet stress, hidden loneliness, feeling behind in life and the self-sabotage cycle each give words to something people live with in silence. three pinned posts (awareness, community, empowerment) anchor the quarter. collabs with Cybersmile, HUMEN and The Okra Project reached communities that often go unheard. every post validates first: it's a pattern with a name, not a personal failure.
