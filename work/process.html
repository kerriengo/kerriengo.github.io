14 / 14 : how i work

process

how a campaign gets built: a messy brainstorm, a feeling, then a flexible template.

01

brainstorm

every month starts with a sticky-note brainstorm. clinical insights, audience questions and concepts get grouped by theme before anything is designed.

02

mood board

a mood board sets the feeling first. for feel lighter 2026 it was warm light, small moments and handwritten notes: what it feels like when something gets a little easier.

03

one quote, three treatments

the same line, tested over three different photos until the image felt as quiet as the words: a night bus, a streetlight with someone sitting beneath it, and the light on its own.

04

a flexible template system

templates keep every post recognizable while the photo, the mood and the voice change. a letter, a summer note and a therapist's take all feel like one account.
