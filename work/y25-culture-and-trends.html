the brief

pop culture gives people a way into feelings they can't name. the TSITP, TLOASG and Spotify Wrapped posts meet people in the shows, songs and moments they already talk about.

my approach

people name their feelings through the things they love. a show, an album or a year in songs gives them words they didn't have. i used these moments as an open door: ask how an episode left you feeling, or make wrapped a way to reflect, then keep the clinical voice light. every post respects the fandom and never talks down to it.

result

linkedin, december 2025: the Spotify Wrapped post earned 2,895 engagements, the top linkedin post in the 2024-to-present dash social report.
